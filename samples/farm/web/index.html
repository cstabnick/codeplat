GOSSIP ACRES

A farm where everyone is talking. Except when Gary's looking.

Prince Oinksalot's Interdimensional Emporium

Cows give milk and hens lay eggs while you watch; both have young and grow old. Click the pig (he is a prince) to shop. Click an animal to hear what it's really thinking. Drag or use the arrow keys to pan, click the field to pluck carrots. Keys: Space, 1/2/4, N, S. Psst: try U after dark, or F.
